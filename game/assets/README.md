# Art assets

The current generated pixel art is a visual prototype for the first playable. The source atlases are kept beside the individual PNGs; `tools/slice_sprite_atlas.gd` trims transparent margins, preserves nearest-neighbor pixel edges, and writes 64×64 sprites/icons.

- The first visual pilot introduced `sprites/player_cyan.png` (32×32) and `sprites/enemy_chaser.png` (24×24). The static hero remains a reference; active player rendering now uses `sprites/player_idle.png` and `sprites/player_walk.png` (4×4 sheets of 32×32 frames) with Aseprite sources in `source/pilot/`. Its original Lightning projectile export remains a visual reference; the active renderer uses the dedicated projectile set below.
- The earlier moss-and-stone ground candidate was excluded after visual review; the active floor is now the quieter pixel-art texture `sprites/environment/survival_ground_pixel.png`.

- `backgrounds/title_skyline.png` is the title-screen city background. It is 640×360 and uses only 30 colors from the validated 32-color Art Bible palette. The generated source is retained at `docs/art/source/title_skyline_generated.png`; `tools/process_title_skyline.gd` resizes and quantizes it reproducibly. This source is a flattened PNG; a layered Aseprite source has not been produced.

- `sprites/catalog/`: dedicated 32×32 icons for all ten weapons, their ten evolved forms, and all eleven relics, including the currently disabled-in-Survival Frost Nova/Frozen Crown pair. The PNGs use at most 16 colors and are wired into choices and run inventory. Their generated reference sheets and editable single-layer Aseprite sources are under `source/catalog_weapons/` and `source/catalog_relics/`; `tools/build_catalog_icon_assets.gd` rebuilds the exports.
- `icons/catalog/`: a matching 16-icon set for six equipment slots, five rarity markers, two resources, skill node, achievement medal, and run record. Equipment, rarity, and reward displays use these exports; the medal/record icons are assets only and do not add those features.
- `sprites/roster/`: nine current enemy/support sprites (24×24), an elite sprinter prototype (24×24), and two abstract boss placeholders (96×96). Survival and the Sandbox preview now use the matching enemy/boss exports. The boss art remains non-canonical while identities and patterns are open; enemy animation frame counts also remain open. Reference sheet and editable sources are under `source/roster/`.
- `sprites/projectiles/`: 24 dedicated 16×16 pixel projectile sprites for the current ten weapons, their evolved forms and enemy/special projectiles. Base and evolved types render through separate batches when they have separate art; each PNG has a matching Aseprite source and the reference atlas is in `source/projectiles/`. Trajectories, trails and impacts remain separate effects; older SVGs are retained as references.
- `sprites/environment/survival_ground_pixel.png` (256×256) is the active, quiet, seamless floor texture, limited to six colors from the Art Bible palette. Its editable source is `source/environment/survival_ground_pixel.aseprite`; `tools/process_survival_ground.gd` rebuilds it from `source/environment/ground_quiet_reference.png`. It changes no collision or combat behavior. The first biome remains **[OUVERT]**.
- `sprites/environment/ground_moss_stone_candidate.png` and its Aseprite source remain a non-active earlier floor candidate; its dense repeated texture distracts from combat.
- `sprites/environment/survival_ruins_pixel.png`: palette-limited pixel-art replacement for the decorative ruins layer. Its editable Aseprite source and generated reference are in `source/environment/`. It changes no collision or gameplay rules; the biome remains **[OUVERT]**.
- `sprites/rewards/chest_closed.png` (32×32) and `xp_orb.png` (16×16): dedicated pixel-art sprites for the existing chest and XP pickup. Their PNG/Aseprite references are in `source/rewards/`; no pickup or reward behavior changed.
- `backgrounds/title_skyline.png` (640×360) and `source/backgrounds/title_skyline.aseprite`: the pixelized neon city backdrop and its single-layer editable Aseprite source. `tools/process_title_skyline.gd` rebuilds both from the generated reference in `docs/art/source/` using 30 colors from the validated master palette.
- `../scripts/title_skyline_lights.gd` overlays three restrained, code-drawn blinking signs on the launch skyline. Their 1.4–2.2 second periods are proposed; the exact timing remains open.
- `sprites/`: player, enemy archetypes and bosses, XP, chest, and legacy relic artwork. The catalog icons above are the active presentation icons for equipment and choices.
- `sprites/survival_ground.svg` remains a non-active visual reference. The previous SVG ruins overlay is also non-active.
- `sprites/weapon_impacts_pixel.png` is a ten-cell, neutral-palette pixel impact atlas used for brief direct-hit flashes across every catalog weapon family; ten individual PNG/Aseprite sources and the generated reference sheet are in `source/vfx/`. Enemy deaths emit a restrained, code-drawn pixel burst from the Art Bible palette. Damage-over-time ticks do not spawn extra flashes. The older color-coded SVG remains as a non-active reference while element/color mapping stays open.
- Moving projectiles receive a short cyan/deep-blue code-drawn trail beneath their sprite in both Survival and the developer Sandbox. The 256 simultaneous-trail cap remains proposed; per-element colors remain open.
- `icons/`: equipment slots, resource rewards, rarity markers, plus skill/achievement/run-record placeholders for future UI.

Gameplay visuals use separate textures where the renderer batches by visual type. These assets do not establish or change gameplay rules, balance, or the still-proposed progression contracts.
