# PNG files without a same-stem Aseprite source
# No active output-source exceptions are currently accepted.
# Historical flattened atlases and their slicer were retired after confirming
# the runtime loads family-specific assets with editable sources.

The PNG references inside game/assets/source/ are builder inputs, not final
sprites; their absence of same-stem Aseprite files is therefore not counted as
an output-source mismatch. SVG assets are their own editable vector sources and
are not expected to have Aseprite files.
